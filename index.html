Side projects

Products and tools I build on my own time.

MOZIC

  • Live
  • Early access

A platform for musicians and the music scene.

Songs, setlists, rehearsals, room bookings and classes in one place, with a public directory of artists, rehearsal rooms, studios, teachers and services by city. It started from my own experience as a musician. I define and prioritise the product, design and build it, make the technical and infrastructure decisions, and talk directly with potential users. It’s in an early stage of adoption.

  • Private by default: only what you publish reaches your public page or the directory.
  • Live mode works offline, as a PWA with local storage, for rehearsal rooms and stages without signal.
  • Imports PDF, Guitar Pro, MIDI and MusicXML, so musicians start from the material they already have.
